# Tensura Questlines

Authored questline content for the Tensura RPG modpack: five major questlines -
with sub-questlines and crossover bridges between them - delivered as FTB
Quests chapters. FTB Quests keeps doing the questbook; this mod is the
content.

## What it does

- **Reincarnation** - the Tensura: Reincarnated core line: first steps,
  nature, skills and the first awakening.
- **Professions** - the Sympathetic Jobs ladder: join, master, unlock job
  slots, and carry several crafts at once.
- **Commerce** - the Lightman's Currency economy line: first coins, first
  shop, trading partners, repeatable bounties, and the tycoon tier.
- **Infinite Depths** - the Infinite Dungeon descent: floors, depth
  milestones, miniboss raids and guardian layer completions, credited to the
  players who earned them.
- **Trials of Power** - the combat and magic line: spells, dragons,
  enchanting, Gateways waves and ordeals, closing on a gate that opens the
  Depths.

Major plotlines are approved before their sub-questlines are authored; the
lines interconnect through named crossover bridges (jobs feed shops, tolls
open the Depths, trials gate the finale).

## Status

Content lands through the major-line issues
([tensura-quests#29](https://github.com/HexaHexes/tensura-quests/issues/29)–[#33](https://github.com/HexaHexes/tensura-quests/issues/33));
until chapters ship, this jar registers nothing beyond its entry point.
